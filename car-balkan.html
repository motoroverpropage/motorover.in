Balkan Self-Drive Road Trip 2026

The Balkan region is a hidden treasure for travellers looking for the quintessential East European experience. Blessed with the moderate Mediterranean climate, a long coastline and mountain ranges, the Balkan peninsula has the best of everything. Postcard cities with a medieval charm, beaches with turquoise waters, natural wonders and thrilling roads in the mountains and along the Dalmatian Coast.

Our road trip covers three countries- Croatia, Bosnia & Herzegovina and Montenegro which were all part of the erstwhile communist nation of SFRY (Yugoslavia). These are Slavic countries with a common culture and influences from Russia, Germany, Austria, Hungary, Italy and even the Turks who have all had political influence over this region through history.

We are offering luxury SUVs for the road trip and it traces a scenic route that covers all the major cities in the region. The driving distances are relaxed and ensure that travellers get ample free time to explore the places we visit. You will thoroughly enjoy the great mix of driving, sightseeing and experiences we have carefully curated.

All you have to do is arrive and drive!
