Georgia Motorcycle Tour 2026

Nestled at the crossroads of Europe and Asia, Georgia is a land of dramatic contrasts, ancient traditions, and breathtaking beauty. Famed for its warm hospitality, unique cuisine, and deep-rooted winemaking heritage, this captivating country offers a tapestry of experiences. From the soaring peaks of the Caucasus Mountains to lush valleys, pristine Black Sea coastlines, and centuries-old monasteries perched on cliffs. Georgia is where nature and culture intertwine in perfect harmony.

Our 8 days/7night motorcycle tour of Georgia invites you to explore the heart and soul of the country. The ride starts and ends in Georgia’s vibrant capital, Tbilisi, where cobbled streets and modern art scenes meet. Our route takes goes west from Tbilisi and then north into the Svaneti region for thrilling rides and mountain passes in the Caucasus mountains. After an overnight journey in the beautiful town of Mestia, we head south to the black sea coast and arrive in the ‘Las Vegas of Georgia’, Batumi. Enjoy the various sights, piazzas and sea views on the rest day in Batumi and try your luck at any of the many casinos the city is famous for. From Batumi we take the southern route and ride eastward back to Tbilisi through mountain passes and exciting dirt trails.

The Georgian motorcycle tour is an arrive and ride adventure. All you have to do is book the tour, get your e-visa for Georgia (not required for USA, UK, Schengen visa holders) and fly into Tbilisi. We are providing a choice of four rental motorcycle you can choose from and your stays for seven nights, all breakfasts and six dinners, airport pickup and drop in Tbilisi are covered in the tour package. Our ground team includes an expert English-speaking motorcycle guide and a MotoRover Indian tour manager on a motorcycle riding at the back of the group. A support car driven by a local mechanic follows the riders and provides breakdown assistance if needed. The support car also carries the group’s luggage and has a first aid kit to ensure that you enjoy the ride with full logistical support.
