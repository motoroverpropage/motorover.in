Our Story

MotoRover was founded by Piyush Sonsale, a former auto journalist with a passion for adventure travel. What started as a dream to explore the world on two wheels has grown into one of India's premier adventure travel operators.

We specialize in international motorcycle tours and self-drive car road trips across Europe, Central Asia, and beyond. Our carefully curated routes take you through some of the most breathtaking landscapes in the world, from the snow-capped mountains of Kyrgyzstan to the coastal roads of Northern Europe.

Our Mission

To provide unforgettable adventure travel experiences that combine the thrill of the open road with cultural immersion, expert guidance, and unparalleled support.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Experienced tour managers and local guides
  • Well-maintained motorcycles and vehicles
  • Small group sizes for personalized experiences
  • 24/7 support during your journey
  • Carefully planned routes with mix of paved and off-road sections
  • Cultural experiences and local interactions
